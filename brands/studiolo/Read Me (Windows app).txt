Studiolo 1.0 — portable Windows app
Private file tools that run on your device.
Created by Daniel Velasquez · danvel1211@gmail.com

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click Studiolo.exe. That's it — Studiolo opens in its own
  window. There is nothing to install, nothing to set up, and no
  admin rights are needed. You can run it straight from your Desktop,
  a folder, or a USB stick, and move or copy the one file anywhere.

  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because
  Studiolo is a new program without a paid publisher certificate. Choose
  "More info", then "Run anyway". This only checks who published the
  file; it does not mean anything is wrong with it.

  If your computer is managed by an employer or school, your IT
  department may need to approve running new programs. That is a
  setting on the computer, not something Studiolo needs installed.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  File combination   merge PDFs and images, arrange pages
  Fill & sign PDF    fill forms and sign with a drawn, typed or uploaded signature
  Edit image         layers, filters, selections and object removal
  Collage maker       photo collages and grids for print or social media
  File converter      convert, inspect and resize images

Nothing you open in Studiolo is ever uploaded or sent anywhere. It
works fully offline. Your name and licence code are remembered on this
computer only.
