Studiolo 1.0
Private file tools that run in your browser.
Created by Daniel Velasquez · danvel1211@gmail.com

HOW TO OPEN
  Windows:        double-click Studiolo.exe
  Mac or Linux:   double-click Studiolo.html

Studiolo opens in your web browser. Nothing is installed, nothing is
uploaded, and it works without an internet connection.

Keep the "resources" folder next to Studiolo.exe and Studiolo.html.
They need it to start.

FIRST TIME ON WINDOWS
Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because
Studiolo.exe is new and not yet signed with a publisher certificate.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You can also open Studiolo.html
instead; it starts exactly the same app.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  File combination   merge PDFs and images, arrange pages
  Fill & sign PDF    fill forms and sign with a drawn, typed or uploaded signature
  Edit image         layers, filters, selections and object removal
  File converter     convert, inspect and resize images
