How it's built

Four principles

01

Private by design

Your files never leave your device. The browser itself is told to block every connection to the internet.

02

Works offline

Everything ships inside the app: the code, the libraries, even the handwriting fonts. No account, no sign-in.

03

Real techniques, no AI

Object removal uses PatchMatch, the classic method behind the original content-aware fill. Predictable and private.

04

Free to use

Built on open libraries with permissive licences (pdf.js, pdf-lib and others), so anyone can use it.
